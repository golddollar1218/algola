Last update: May 17, 2026

Algola is designed as an educational, informational, analytical, and technology-focused tool. Algola does not provide financial services, investment advice, brokerage, custody, exchange, payment, banking, portfolio management, or trading services. Our privacy practices are designed to support this limited role.

We aim to collect only the data reasonably necessary to operate, secure, support, and improve the Services. We do not request, store, view, back up, or recover private keys, seed phrases, or wallet recovery phrases. We do not need withdrawal-enabled API access, and users should not connect API keys with withdrawal permissions.

By accessing or using the Services, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy. Where applicable law requires consent for a specific processing activity, such as optional cookies or marketing communications, we will request such consent separately.

If you do not agree with this Privacy Policy, you should not access or use the Services.
